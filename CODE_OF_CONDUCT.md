# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Offer and accept feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Disruptive or abusive communication.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
