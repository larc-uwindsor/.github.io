01Who we are

Engineering the
autonomous future.

Lancer Autonomous Robotics Club is a student-led robotics team at the University of Windsor. We bring together engineering, computer science, and business students to design, build, and deploy high-performance autonomous vehicles for national and international competitions.

04Competitions

Proving it
on the course.

Our current target is the 34th annual Intelligent Ground Vehicle Competition (IGVC), June 4-8, 2027, at Oakland University in Rochester, Michigan, where student-built vehicles navigate an outdoor obstacle course fully autonomously.

As we grow into aerial and marine platforms, we're eyeing RoboSub and the AEAC Unmanned Vehicle Systems Competition.

SupportPartnership

Powered by
partners.

LARC runs on sponsorship. Partnership tiers run from $100 to $5,000+, with benefits including logo placement on the vehicle and apparel, social recognition, and recruitment access at higher tiers. We also welcome in-kind donations of parts, materials, and machining time.

05Get involved

Bring your
curiosity.

Membership is open to all University of Windsor undergrad and grad students, across every discipline. Engineering, computer science, and business students build the vehicles and the organization together. The membership year runs September to August, with monthly general meetings and weekly subteam meetings.
