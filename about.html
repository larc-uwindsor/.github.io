01Who we are

Lancer Autonomous Robotics Club is a student-led robotics team at the University of Windsor dedicated to advancing autonomous systems across ground, aerial, and marine platforms.

Our team brings together multidisciplinary engineering, computer science, and business students to design, build, and deploy high-performance autonomous vehicles for national and international competitions.

Our mission

To cultivate top-tier engineering talent, drive hands-on technical innovation, and establish a legacy of excellence in autonomous robotics.
